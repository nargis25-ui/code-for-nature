# Problem Statement

Environmental sustainability is an important challenge, but many people do not clearly understand how their everyday activities contribute to environmental impact.

People consume electricity and water, generate electronic waste, and increasingly use AI tools. However, there is no simple and user-friendly way for everyday users to track these activities together, understand their environmental impact, and receive personalized suggestions for improvement.

The gap we aim to address is the lack of an accessible platform that connects everyday consumption with understandable sustainability insights and actionable recommendations.

Our project aims to make sustainability measurable, understandable, and actionable for everyday users.
