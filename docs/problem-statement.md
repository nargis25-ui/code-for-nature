# Problem Statement

## Background

Climate change and environmental sustainability are important challenges. Individuals and organisations need understandable information to make more informed decisions about resource usage.

Electricity, water, electronic waste, and AI-related computing are different areas that users may want to monitor. However, collecting this information and interpreting it can be difficult without a simple, accessible tool.

## Problem

Users need a convenient way to record and review sustainability-related usage information. They may also need clearer guidance on how to make more responsible choices.

## Proposed Opportunity

EcoAI explores a simple digital approach to sustainability tracking. It aims to bring usage records and sustainability awareness into one interface while providing a foundation for future reporting and environmental estimates.

## Target Users

- Individuals interested in tracking resource usage.
- Students and educational communities.
- Organisations exploring sustainability practices.

## Project Objective

Develop a usable prototype that supports recording sustainability-related usage data and encourages informed environmental decisions.

## Scope and Limitations

The prototype does not independently measure all environmental impacts. The accuracy of any environmental estimate depends on input quality, units, calculation methods, and reliable supporting data.
