# Future Scope

EcoAI is a sustainability-tracking prototype. The following ideas are possible future improvements and should not be considered completed features unless they are implemented and tested.

## 1. Improved Environmental Estimates

- Add documented electricity-related emission factors.
- Support region-specific factors where reliable data is available.
- Clearly show units, assumptions, and estimation methods.
- Distinguish measured values from calculated estimates.

## 2. User Accounts and Organisations

- Add secure authentication and role-based access.
- Support separate individual and organisational workspaces.
- Allow authorised teams to manage and review their records.

## 3. Reports and Progress Tracking

- Add downloadable sustainability reports.
- Compare usage across selected periods.
- Display trends when sufficient records are available.
- Allow users to export their own data.

## 4. AI Sustainability Assistance

- Develop a prompt-efficiency assistant that helps users write concise, useful prompts.
- Provide recommendations with clear explanations.
- Evaluate suggestions for usefulness and accuracy before presenting them as reliable guidance.

## 5. Integrations

- Explore smart-meter and IoT integrations.
- Connect to reliable environmental datasets.
- Investigate integrations that provide verifiable AI or computing energy information.

## 6. Engagement and Accessibility

- Add optional sustainability goals and progress indicators.
- Improve accessibility and mobile responsiveness.
- Collect user feedback to guide future improvements.

## 7. Future Validation

Before expanding the system, test data accuracy, security, usability, and the reliability of environmental calculations. Any reported environmental benefit should be supported by evidence.
